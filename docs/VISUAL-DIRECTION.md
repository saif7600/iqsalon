# Visual Direction

## Authority

The supplied July 30, 2026 visual reference is the primary direction. It is a
language reference, not a pixel-copy specification.

## Translation

AtiqSalon uses:

- A calm left navigation rail with clear active state.
- A compact utility header for branch, search, quick create, notifications,
  language, theme, and user context.
- Dense but aligned operational workspaces.
- Four or five primary metrics per desktop row.
- A schedule-first owner and reception experience.
- Contextual AI insight panels that do not dominate the workspace.
- Dedicated mobile patterns rather than compressed desktop screens.
- Restrained violet as the brand accent, balanced by indigo, teal, emerald,
  amber, and rose operational colors.

## Brand

The identity combines a compact connected-node or abstract-petal mark with the
AtiqSalon AI wordmark and optional “Business Operating System” label. It avoids
gendered silhouettes, scissors as a primary symbol, decorative mandalas, and
gold-heavy treatment.

## Inclusive content

Examples represent haircuts, beard trims, hair color, facials, manicures,
massage, grooming, treatments, spa service, and home service. Names, staff,
customers, and service imagery must be balanced across genders and business
types.

## Operational hierarchy

1. Current context and urgent state
2. Primary action
3. Schedule or task workspace
4. Financial and operating summaries
5. AI evidence and recommendations
6. Historical activity

Marketing content does not appear inside operational modules.

## Prohibited patterns

- Female-only product positioning
- Pink-dominant styling
- Generic admin-dashboard templates
- Glassmorphism as the primary surface language
- Neon, crypto, gamer, or cyberpunk treatment
- Excessive cards, gradients, pills, shadows, and rounded corners
- Fake charts, statistics, testimonials, logos, or AI claims
- Oversized marketing headings in the portal
